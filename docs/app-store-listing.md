### Description

```
QuietGPT is a private AI that runs on your iPhone. It does not send your words to a server. It works in Airplane Mode.

Ask questions, write, plan and think in a chat that stays on your iPhone. Then use the same AI in any app with the QuietGPT keyboard.

LOCAL, NOT CLOUD
- The AI runs on your iPhone with Apple Foundation Models.
- It works with no internet connection.
- No account, no server, no analytics and no ads.
- Your chats stay on your iPhone, and you can search them.
- Backups do not include your chats, so they do not move to a new iPhone.
- Face ID lock and Delete all data are built in.

APPLE NEURAL ENGINE
In our recorded tests on iPhone 16 Pro Max, chat replies ran on the Apple Neural Engine. iOS selects the hardware. The method, the data and the limits of the tests are public in the repository.

PRIVATE CHAT
- Replies stream on your iPhone, with clear formatting for lists and code.
- Ask for ideas, explanations, drafts, summaries and plans.

AI IN ANY APP
- Keyboard: It types like the iOS keyboard, with accents, cursor control and all emoji. It does not ask for full access.
- One tap: Make any message kinder, shorter, longer or more formal, fix it, or translate it to English or Spanish.
- My rules: Up to 10 short rules that QuietGPT follows each time, for example "Use short sentences."
- People: Save how you write to one person.
- Style packs: Send your rules and styles to other people as a file. Your people stay on your iPhone.

OPEN SOURCE
The full source code is public under the MIT License. You do not have to accept our privacy claims without proof. Read the code, build the app yourself, or turn on Airplane Mode and test it.
https://github.com/pdsdm/QuietGPT

REQUIREMENTS
iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone Air, or iPhone 16 or later, with iOS 26 or later. Apple Intelligence must be on and must finish its download. QuietGPT does not include this download.

SUPPORT
Questions and bug reports: https://github.com/pdsdm/QuietGPT/issues

NOTE: AI text can contain errors. Read each reply before you use it. QuietGPT does not give medical, legal or financial advice.

Privacy policy: https://github.com/pdsdm/QuietGPT/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
```
